# Stage Log

## Progress

Record completed work in chronological order.

## Evidence

Record each claim as `static`, `runtime`, or `inference` with its source path, command, and artifact
path. Do not record runtime success from process startup alone.

## Changes

List changed paths and why each change belongs to this stage.

## Problems

Record failures, rejected approaches, and unresolved questions.

## Handoff

Summarize what the next stage can rely on.
